INDUSTRIAL WATER & WASTEWATER SOLUTIONS

Supply & Export of Water Treatment Systems

Rhythm Texchem provides industrial and commercial water treatment solutions designed around application needs, feed-water quality and operating requirements. Our portfolio complements our textile chemicals and specialty chemical business with solutions for purification, wastewater treatment, water recycling, hardness control and filtration.

Industrial RO Plant

Reverse osmosis systems for reducing dissolved salts, minerals and other impurities to produce purified water for industrial processes.

  • Industrial process water
  • Textile & chemical applications
  • Customized capacity and configuration

ETP Plant

Effluent Treatment Plants for industrial wastewater treatment, helping reduce pollutants and support responsible wastewater management before compliant discharge or reuse.

  • Textile effluent
  • Chemical industry wastewater
  • Application-based treatment design

STP Plant

Sewage Treatment Plants for domestic and commercial wastewater, with solutions designed to support water recycling and reuse for suitable non-potable applications.

  • Residential & commercial projects
  • Hotels, institutions & facilities
  • Water reuse focused solutions

Water Softener Plant

Water softening systems designed to reduce hardness-causing calcium and magnesium ions, helping control scale formation and protect water-handling equipment.

  • Industrial applications
  • Boiler & utility water support
  • Customized softening solutions

WTP – Water Treatment Plant

Water Treatment Plants for raw-water purification, targeting suspended solids, turbidity and other contaminants according to the source-water and end-use requirements.

  • Raw-water treatment
  • Industrial & commercial use
  • Integrated filtration options

DM Plant – Demineralization Plant

Demineralization systems for applications requiring low-mineral and high-purity water, using ion-exchange based treatment configurations selected for the process requirement.

  • High-purity process water
  • Chemical & industrial applications
  • Customized system design

Chemical Dosing System

Controlled chemical dosing solutions for water and wastewater treatment processes, helping maintain accurate chemical addition and consistent treatment performance.

  • Precise chemical injection
  • Water & wastewater applications
  • Application-specific dosing arrangements

Water Treatment Chemicals

Water-treatment chemical solutions for applications such as scale control, corrosion management and process performance. Product selection should be based on water analysis and operating conditions.

  • Industrial water systems
  • RO & utility applications
  • Technical product selection support

Cartridge Filter

Fine filtration solutions for reducing suspended particles and sediments, providing an additional filtration stage in water treatment and process-water systems.

  • Industrial filtration
  • Pre-filtration applications
  • Easy replacement formats
